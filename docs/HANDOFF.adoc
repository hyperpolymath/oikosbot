== Non-negotiable boundaries

* Remain advisory/alpha. No semver release, stable image promotion, Marketplace
  launch, regulator enablement or auto-fix authority without explicit owner approval
  and the launch receipts. A code merge is not a release approval.
* Preserve exit 2 for no requested check performed. Do not make missing evidence,
  malformed input, missing tools or unavailable integrations green.
* Expert tables remain Estimated/Unknown. Recognition of a code pattern, a range,
  or an enum label cannot manufacture calibration, a probability or policy authority.
* Pareto dominance permits no losses; epsilon suppresses small wins, not losses.
  Invalid input is Indeterminate. The interval contract remains a separate prototype.
* `Feasible` witnesses only the declared numeric constraints. No claim of real-world
  adequacy, consent, authenticated evidence, budget ownership or ecological benefit.
* Rust workspace is standalone. Do not reintroduce the separate economics DSL or
  fleet Sustainabot identity. Security/native Eclexia seams report unavailable until
  real adapters are integrated. App and VeriSimDB remain operationally unverified.
* Preserve existing licence notices. Do not bulk-copy template scaffolds, badges,
  ABI proofs or metadata formats and claim conformance. Consult the applicability
  tensions and exact source revisions recorded in the audit.

== Owner decisions (not chores delegated back from automatable work)

. Choose the first useful functional unit and representative workload: for example,
  one successful request at specified correctness/latency, or one verified build.
  Name the first one or two repositories/pilots and the reference baseline.
. Choose the observation boundary and horizon: operational electricity only versus
  embodied/upstream effects; region/grid assumptions; per-operation versus portfolio
  budgets. State which omissions must be visible on every report.
. Decide non-compensatory limits and who may accept a genuine trade-off. Financial
  savings must not silently override an ecological ceiling. Unknown/unmeasured limits
  remain unknown; do not invent numbers to unblock the agent.
. Approve profiling resources/access and an initial advisory-only pilot when its
  artifact checks pass. If measured power is unavailable, accept a clearly labeled
  proxy experiment—not a false energy calibration.
. Keep general launch/regulator/App deployment on hold. Review a later explicit
  release proposal with receipts; no release action is required merely to merge
  the hardening work.

An agent can draft options and run the engineering checks. It cannot decide these
valuation/authority boundaries on the owner's behalf or obtain hardware readings
from an environment that does not expose them.
