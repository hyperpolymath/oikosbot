= Evidence-scoped Pareto judgements: type contract and research question
:toc:
:stem: latexmath

Status: *designed*, with an experimental Rust runtime refinement prototype in
`crates/oikosbot-pareto/src/contract.rs`. No novel calculus or new theorem is
claimed. No upstream proofs were executed in this audit, and none have been
transported into the Rust implementation.

== The apparent paradox

Economics and ecology do not need to be collapsed into one ordered scalar.
Resource allocation can take ecological limits as constraints rather than as
prices. Pareto order is a *partial* order: incomparability is legitimate output,
not a type error to hide. A Pareto frontier alone cannot choose among incomparable
points, make a feasible set nonempty, or decide whose welfare matters.

Three different questions must stay separate:

. *Feasibility*: are non-negotiable constraints satisfied?
. *Comparison*: does one admissible alternative improve on another under a
  declared model, or do they trade off?
. *Authorization*: who may accept the trade-off, with what evidence, safeguards,
  expiry and appeal mechanism?

Ordinary products, sums, refinements, dependent pairs and indexed relations can
represent these today. A new primitive type is not needed merely to store a
vector or prevent an unvalidated value from entering a checked API. The research
opportunity is in *evidence-preserving transport, composition and revision* of
these judgements, including the consequences of characterization choices.

== A precise bounded claim

Let a context `C` specify useful functional unit, workload distribution, system
boundary (operational/embodied effects, upstream/downstream), horizon, population,
objective schema/units/directions, model revision and policy revision. Let `E`
be evidence and `W(C,E)` the nonempty set of worlds consistent with it.
A robust no-worse judgement for head `h` versus base `b` requires, after orienting
all axes as costs:

[stem]
++++
\forall w \in W(C,E),\ \forall i,\ f_i(h,w) \le f_i(b,w).
++++

For the prototype's stronger fixed-driver improvement require:

[stem]
++++
\exists j,\ \forall w \in W(C,E),\ f_j(b,w)-f_j(h,w) > \epsilon_j.
++++

This differs from `for every world there exists an improved axis`: preserve the
quantifier order. Constraints also hold universally, not just at mean estimates.
If `W` is empty, return inconsistent evidence, not a vacuously certified win.
Applying the claim to reality additionally requires actual-world membership and
adequacy of the observation/valuation model. No type checker can infer those
from syntax or a confidence enum.

A schematic dependent record (specification, not checked code) is:

[source,text]
----
ScopedJudgement C E b h =
  admissibleWorldsNonempty
  × functionalEquivalenceUnder C b h
  × hardConstraintsHold C E h
  × (RobustImprovement C E b h
     + RobustRegression C E b h
     + EstablishedTradeOff C E b h
     + Neutral C E b h
     + Unresolved C E b h)
  × evidenceReceipts
  × excludedObservablesAndResidualRisk
----

`Authorized C E judgement` must be a *different* type supplied by an actual
policy/consent authority. Never provide `Confidence -> Authorized` or
`tradeoff_heading_present -> Authorized`. Even measured data can be irrelevant
to the claim; calibration does not make an obligation.

== Runtime prototype and current CLI

The experimental module implements only finite interval-box numeric checks:

* `Interval::new` rejects reversed, NaN and infinite bounds.
* `Observation::new` requires one value/evidence record per dimension, nonempty
  context, units and source names, distinct axes and finite per-axis tolerances.
* Comparisons require equal contexts, including ordered units and policy/model
  versions. No implicit currency, carbon-unit or workload conversion exists.
* For a minimizing axis, head's upper bound must be at most base's lower bound
  to establish robust no-worsening. Interval overlap is normally `Unresolved`,
  not a point-estimate victory. Box independence is conservative; real joint
  evidence can support more precise conclusions.
* `admit()` constructs a private `Feasible` witness only when the whole interval
  satisfies every declared ceiling/floor. A cheaper point outside a carbon cap
  cannot buy admission. This checks declared numbers, not real-world feasibility.
* Evidence records remain attached. Their source strings are not authenticated,
  and their confidence fields do not confer authority. There is deliberately no
  deserializer or public constructor for the checked witnesses and no conversion
  from these witnesses to an auto-fix/merge capability.

The existing CLI has no workload-equivalence or evidence-ingestion adapter, so
it does *not* use this module to authorize anything. Its old point-vector scores
remain advisory proxies. Missing evidence in an explicitly requested gate is
exit 2, not success and not an ecological violation.

== Two concrete mathematical failure modes

=== Epsilon cycles

Previously `a` could dominate `b` while being worse by epsilon on some axes.
At epsilon 1, all-minimize vectors `(0,1,2)`, `(2,0,1)`, `(1,2,0)` form a cycle:
each wins by 2 on one axis and loses by 1 on two. The frontier is empty; the old
fallback awarded 100 anyway. The corrected relation allows *no losses* and
requires at least one win greater than epsilon. Its intended real-number
relation is transitive and irreflexive. Exhaustive finite-grid tests are not a
machine-checked proof for the floating-point implementation.

=== Scalarization forgets what is needed for safety

Two points with equal weighted score can lie on opposite sides of a hard carbon
cap. For equal weights, `(carbon=12,cost=0)` and `(carbon=6,cost=6)` both score 12;
a cap of 10 separates them. A function seeing only 12 cannot decide both cases
correctly. Retain the original vector, units, constraints and evidence rather
than claiming a scalar is a lossless summary. Positive-weight scalarization may
select supported frontier points; it cannot recover every nonconvex frontier or
supply normative weights.

== Relation to the estate (source inspection, not proof reuse)

* *Eclexia*: dimensional resource types, hard `requires` constraints, adaptive
  solutions and shadow prices are directly relevant. Prices are marginal values
  relative to constraints/model; they do not cancel ecological ceilings. The
  OikosBot native adapter remains unavailable and the builtin evaluator does not
  execute arbitrary `.ecl`. Do not imply full Eclexia semantics are integrated.
* *Residual Evidence Types*: `world + observation equality + E(world)` is the
  closest formulation for claims true across all admissible candidates. Preserve
  nonemptiness and actual-world membership rather than counting candidates as
  probabilities. The library explicitly describes ordinary dependent constructions.
* *Epistemic Types*: warrant versus sound warrant, receiver-relative claim meaning,
  and evidence transport address the central trust-boundary issue. A report or
  receipt must not silently become factive knowledge after serialization.
* *Echo Types*: use fibers/residues to expose what a scalar projection forgets.
  Retaining the source witness differs from asserting the scalar identifies it.
  An aggregate score is not an ecological proof; a resource grade is not an Echo.
* *Tropical Types*: composition laws for resource bounds are relevant, but must
  differ by resource and execution model. Sequential energy can add; concurrent
  latency may take a max under stated scheduling assumptions; peak memory has
  lifetime/overlap constraints. Do not use a single semiring for all axes by fiat.
* *Choreographic Types*: distributed measurement/decision workflows could need
  provenance transport across causal cuts. K-CUT is presented as open upstream;
  do not use it as an established theorem or a current launch dependency.
* *Absolute Zero*: declare the observation model and excluded observables, and
  separate null effect from null disclosure. Identity of program output is not
  zero physical energy, and not evidence of no external ecological effect.
* *JanusKey*: inversion metadata can restore modeled file state; it cannot undo
  consumed electricity, emissions, disclosure or a decision's social impact.
  Restoring code is containment/rollback, not full external-world reversibility.
* *Proven-Tests*: paired silent/firing fixtures and evidence grades are adopted
  as testing discipline. Rust examples are not reclassified as Idris2 proofs.

== Falsifiable research programme (not a launch prerequisite)

. Mechanize the minimal context-indexed order and feasibility record in one
  existing estate prover. Prove irreflexivity/transitivity; exhibit incomparable
  alternatives and an empty feasible set. State the numeric domain explicitly.
. Prove soundness of interval comparison relative to box membership, then give
  counterexamples showing where correlations make that abstraction incomplete.
. Establish a no-smuggling theorem for evidence transport: changing boundary,
  workload, units, observer or policy requires an explicit validated mapping.
. Investigate composition under shared budgets and overlapping effects. Two
  locally feasible changes need not be jointly feasible; duplicated evidence
  and double-spent budgets need linear/affine accounting or a ledger.
. Define revision/retraction: invalidated calibration must invalidate dependent
  judgements, not leave stale green badges. Explore monotonicity under stronger
  evidence and its failure when policy/observation boundaries change.
. Compare against refinement/dependent types, robust multiobjective optimization,
  multi-criteria decision analysis, resource/effect systems and decision theory.
  Claim novelty only after a literature review and a separation result identifying
  what this assembly does that those existing constructions do not.

Do not let the research name imply that economics and ecology are now reconciled
universally. An appropriate working description is *evidence-scoped Pareto
judgements with non-compensatory constraints*, not a proved “sustainability type”.

== Characterization and adoption

Publish the model card alongside every report: whose objectives, workload and
functional equivalence, time horizon, geography, lifecycle boundary, uncertainty,
omitted effects, normalization/reference cohort and policy authority. Show the
vector/frontier and limitations before any aggregate ranking. Provide contestability,
manual review and retraction; do not rank people or infer moral worth from repo CI.

Absolute ecological budgets, distributional effects and rebound (cheaper work
causing more work) can defeat per-unit efficiency claims. “Pareto-optimal” means
nondominated among the compared alternatives under this model, *not* sustainable,
fair, globally optimal or fit to launch. This characterization is part of the
safety contract, not marketing copy added after the mathematics.

Concrete Eclexia implementation inspected at the pinned revision:
`runtime/eclexia-runtime/src/adaptive.rs`, especially
`SolutionCandidate::meets_budget` and `AdaptiveDecisionEngine::select_best`.
It already filters candidates by resource budget *before* weighted/shadow-price
selection. Thus feasibility-before-preference is not a new OikosBot invention.
That implementation also uses ordinary floating-point comparisons; those are
not a substitute for finite-value validation or measurement evidence. OikosBot's
experimental boundary rejects non-finite values rather than inheriting a
comparison's fallback-to-equality behavior. No upstream patch or proof is claimed.
